Master Network Capacity & Routing Agreement

This Master Network Capacity & Routing Agreement (the “Agreement”), effective as of ____________________ (the “Effective Date”), is entered into by Rare Structure LLC (the “Operator”) and the participating entity, ______________________________ d/b/a ____________________________, hereby defined as the “Principal”.

1. Core Infrastructure & Deliverables

1.1 The Capacity Seat. Operator maintains and operates a proprietary, out-of-core data infrastructure and private routing network designed to detect corporate inflection points and originate facilitated digital connections. Subject to the full execution of this Agreement and the irrevocable clearance of the Capacity Seat Fee, Operator grants Principal a single, reserved “Capacity Seat” within the active network.

1.2 Continuous Calibration. The reservation of the Capacity Seat strictly entitles Principal to the continuous algorithmic calibration of their submitted parameters against external market signals. Principal acknowledges that Operator is actively running synthesis between Principal’s operational profile and broader market data, but Operator assumes no obligation to guarantee a specific volume of human interaction or deal flow yield.

1.3 Discretion, Execution & Infrastructure Autonomy. Operator retains absolute sole discretion over the methods, data architecture, protocols, and front-facing enterprise networks utilized to execute this Agreement. Operator shall bear all costs and expenses associated with the development, deployment, and maintenance of its proprietary architecture, including all computational data ingestion, high-dimensional scalar processing, vectorized entity resolution, and outbound routing infrastructure. Principal bears no financial responsibility for Operator’s operational, technical, or outreach overhead beyond the fees explicitly defined in Section 5.

1.4 Non-Brokerage & Liability Acknowledgment. The Parties acknowledge and agree that Operator acts solely as a data-routing infrastructure and lead generation service. Operator does not, and will not, advise on specific contract terms, negotiate teaming agreements, or act as a registered employment agency, staffing broker, or legal advisor. Operator makes no representations or warranties regarding the viability, solvency, or intent of any routed entity. Principal retains sole responsibility for all due diligence, contracting, and operational decisions.

1.5 Non-Exclusive Engagement. Principal acknowledges and agrees that this Agreement does not grant Principal exclusive rights to Operator’s proprietary data pipeline, routing network, or routed deal flow. Operator retains the absolute, unrestricted right to enter into similar agreements with other market participants, including competing firms, and to route any originated opportunity to any party at Operator’s sole strategic discretion.

2. Data Ingestion & Engine Parameters

2.1 Parameter Ingestion. Upon the irrevocable clearance of the Capacity Seat Fee, Principal shall be granted the facility to submit their operational parameters, underwriting criteria, and target market preferences (the “Capacity Profile”). Operator shall synthesize these passive inputs to continuously calibrate the routing allocation engine. Failure by Principal to submit a Capacity Profile does not entitle Principal to any refund or extension of the term.

2.2 Calibration Entities. Principal’s submission may include the identities of specific market entities. Principal explicitly acknowledges and agrees that any specific entities provided are submitted strictly as illustrative data points for comparative clustering and vector alignment. Operator assumes absolutely no obligation to monitor, target, solicit, or route connections regarding any specific entity submitted within this calibration data.

2.3 Exclusion Roster. Principal may, concurrent with the execution of this Agreement or during the parameter ingestion phase, provide Operator with an “Exclusion Roster” consisting of Principal’s existing, active accounts or relationships. Operator will actively suppress these specific entities from the routing pipeline to prevent conflicting connections.

3. Algorithmic Routing & Alignment

3.1 Routed Connections. For the purposes of this Agreement, a “Routed Connection” is strictly defined as a digital facilitation connecting Principal with a representative of a third-party market participant (a “Network Counterparty”).

3.2 Algorithmic Alignment. Operator retains absolute engineering control over the data pipeline inputs, matching models, and threshold criteria. A connection is routed when, in Operator’s sole and strategic discretion, a Network Counterparty’s expressed or inferred operational requirements, preferences, or systemic market positioning align with the Principal’s submitted Capacity Profile.

4. Intellectual Property & Architectural Opacity

4.1 Zero Reporting Obligation. Operator shall have no obligation to disclose, explain, justify, or provide reporting on the internal algorithmic logic, scoring mechanisms, or specific data inputs utilized to determine any alignment or routing decision.

4.2 Proprietary Architecture. The underlying routing engine relies on highly opaque, out-of-core computational processing, multi-dimensional vector embeddings, and non-linear data synthesis that cannot be linearly audited or extracted. Principal acknowledges that these computational mechanics constitute strictly confidential, proprietary intellectual property intrinsic to the foundational valuation of Operator’s enterprise. Any audit, disclosure, or reverse-engineering of this architecture, whether intentional or inadvertent, would trigger severe, compounding material degradation to Operator’s market position and structural leverage.

5. Economics & Consideration

5.1 The Capacity Seat Fee. In consideration for the reservation of a Capacity Seat and the continuous algorithmic calibration of Principal’s submitted parameters against external market data, Principal shall remit a flat, non-refundable ____________________ (the “Capacity Seat Fee”) for the Primary Term. This fee fully accounts for Operator’s out-of-core data ingestion, vector alignment, and all structural costs associated with continuous market monitoring and digital facilitation.

5.2 Fee Acknowledgement. Principal explicitly acknowledges that this fee strictly secures the Capacity Seat and the ongoing calibration process; it does not purchase a guaranteed quota of routing yield. Should market conditions fail to produce aligned Network Counterparties, the Capacity Seat Fee remains fully earned and non-refundable.

5.3 Zero Yield Encumbrance. Operator evaluates and facilitates alignments strictly based on the prepaid Capacity Seat Fee. Operator takes a 0.00% take-rate and lays no claim to any equity, success fees, basis points, or secondary compensation resulting from transactions executed between Principal and any Network Counterparty.

6. Market Reality & Counterparty Friction

6.1 Market Volatility & Autonomy. Principal explicitly acknowledges that Operator’s infrastructure functions within a dynamic, highly unpredictable market environment. Operator makes no representations or warranties regarding macroeconomic conditions, transaction volume, or the velocity of market signals.

6.2 Counterparty Friction. Operator strictly facilitates digital introductions; Operator exercises no control over the autonomy, responsiveness, or ultimate intent of any Network Counterparty. Should algorithmic alignment occur, but the Network Counterparty declines engagement, ceases communication, or refuses facilitation, such events constitute standard market friction. Operator is completely absolved of any liability or obligation regarding the ultimate responsiveness, communication, or conversion of any Routed Connection.

7. Term & Expiration

7.1 Primary Term. This Agreement governs a singular, discrete ____________-day period (the “Primary Term”), commencing strictly upon the irrevocable clearance of the Capacity Seat Fee.

7.2 Expiration of Term. Upon expiration of the Primary Term, the Principal’s reserved Capacity Seat, along with the continuous calibration of their submitted parameters against external market signals, strictly conclude. The terms, data inputs, and system inclusion governed herein do not auto-renew, carry forward, or implicitly apply to any future engagements or subsequent payments unless expressly executed via a net-new formal agreement and the remittance of a new Capacity Seat Fee.
