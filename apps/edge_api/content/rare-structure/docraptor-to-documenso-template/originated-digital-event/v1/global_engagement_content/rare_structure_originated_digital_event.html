Strategic Origination Agreement

This Strategic Origination Agreement (the “Agreement”), effective as of ____________________ (the “Effective Date”), is entered into by Rare Structure LLC (the “Operator”) and the participating entity, ______________________________ d/b/a ____________________________, hereby defined as the “Principal”.

1. Infrastructure & Origination Engine

1.1 System Architecture. Operator has created, operates, and deploys proprietary data-processing architecture designed to ingest, resolve, and detect unstructured corporate inflection points, including federal contract awards and prime contractor ecosystem signals. Operator utilizes this specialized data-engineering infrastructure to identify and originate off-market commercial partnership and targeted audience opportunities.

1.2 Strategic Origination. Principal engages Operator on a business development basis to secure an active routing node aligned, as assessed by Operator, to the Principal’s operational parameters and target verticals. This engagement is explicitly framed as an un-brokered origination channel driven by algorithmic discovery configured, applied, and acted upon at Operator’s sole and strategic discretion.

2. The Originated Digital Event

2.1 The Event. Leveraging the System Architecture outlined above, Operator will execute an outbound origination sequence to secure, manage, and host a live video conference (the “Originated Digital Event” or “Event”) on behalf of Principal. In exchange for the fully paid Prepaid Event Fee of ____________________ (the “Prepaid Event Fee”), Operator will provide the technical hosting platform and deliver the Event.

2.2 Event Duration. The total scheduled duration of the Event shall not exceed ____________ minutes. Within this timeframe, Principal and Operator may freely allocate the session between presentations, conversational discussions, and Q&A without strict internal time limits.

3. Addressable Universe & Outreach

3.1 Filtering Parameters. To define the audience for the Event, Principal will submit their ideal data criteria (the “Filtering Parameters”). These parameters may include, but are not limited to, NAICS codes, PSC codes, total obligated value, and specific classifications such as prime contractor or sub-awardee status.

3.2 Functional Decision-Makers. Rather than requiring exact job-title string matches, Principal will outline the desired executive functions (e.g., Operations, Procurement, C-Suite). Operator will focus outreach on the “Functional Decision-Makers” within the organizations, acknowledging that literal job titles vary widely and are evaluated based on operational relevance rather than exact terminology.

3.3 The Approved Target Audience. Operator will process the Filtering Parameters and present Principal with a summary of the resulting data pool. Once Principal signs off on this summary, this data pool officially becomes the “Approved Target Audience.” Principal explicitly acknowledges that they are approving the data parameters and general scale of the audience, and will not receive any personally identifiable information (PII) or specific entity names during this stage.

3.4 Data Imperfections & Outreach Control. Principal acknowledges that third-party data, federal registries, and self-reported employment information are inherently imperfect. Operator will use commercially reasonable efforts to align its outreach with the Approved Target Audience, but Operator is not liable for standard data decay, misclassified data codes, or individuals who misrepresent their roles. Operator retains complete, proprietary control over the actual outreach mechanics, channels, and communication methods used to secure registrations.

4. Timeline & Scheduling

4.1 Scheduling & Pipeline Runway. Following the clearance of the Prepaid Event Fee and approval of the Target Audience, Principal has thirty (30) days to select a mutually agreeable date for the Event. To ensure Operator has adequate time to execute the outreach pipeline and build audience demand, the selected Event date must be scheduled no less than thirty (30) days out from the day the date is officially confirmed.

4.2 The Execution Window. The Event must take place within a maximum of ninety (90) days from the Effective Date. If Principal fails to select a valid date within the initial 30-day scheduling window, causes unreasonable delays, or fails to execute the Event before the 90-day drop-dead date, Operator is fully absolved of its obligations, and the Prepaid Event Fee is entirely forfeited with no refund.

4.3 Cancellations & Market Damage. Principal acknowledges that Operator incurs significant upfront costs and expends unrecoverable market goodwill to assemble the Approved Target Audience. In the event of a Principal-driven cancellation or reschedule request after outreach has commenced, the Prepaid Event Fee is strictly non-refundable, and Operator is fully released from the Minimum Headcount guarantee. Operator may, at its sole and absolute discretion, offer to reschedule the Event subject to a supplemental “Reprovisioning Fee” to cover the cost of running a secondary outreach pipeline.

5. Minimum Headcount & Make-Good Obligations

5.1 The Minimum Headcount. Operator guarantees that a minimum of ____________ attendees will log into the Event live (the “Minimum Headcount”).

5.2 The Headcount Snapshot. To ensure an objective and undisputed audit of the Minimum Headcount, the official count of Live Attendees will be taken exactly five (5) minutes past the mutually agreed-upon start time. This snapshot is the sole metric used to determine if the Minimum Headcount was met. The snapshot will be recorded regardless of whether Principal’s speakers log in late, experience technical difficulties, or fail to engage the audience.

5.3 The Supplemental Event. If the number of live attendees at the Headcount Snapshot falls below the Minimum Headcount, Principal is not entitled to a refund, pro-ration, or chargeback of the Prepaid Event Fee. Instead, Operator’s sole responsibility is to organize and host a “Supplemental Event” within ninety (90) days to deliver the remaining balance of live attendees required to meet the Minimum Headcount. The scheduling of any Supplemental Event remains subject to the same 30-day minimum lead time requirement outlined in Section 4.1.

6. Post-Event Data Delivery

6.1 The Attendee Roster. Within ninety-six (96) hours following the conclusion of the Originated Digital Event (and any Supplemental Event), and strictly subject to the expressed consent of the attendees, Operator will deliver an “Attendee Roster” to Principal. This roster will contain the professional contact information solely of the individuals who actually attended the call live. Principal retains full rights to use this roster for post-event sales follow-ups and direct communication.
