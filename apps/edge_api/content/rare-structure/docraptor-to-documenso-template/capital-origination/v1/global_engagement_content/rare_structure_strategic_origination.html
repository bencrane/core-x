RARE STRUCTURE

Strategic Origination Agreement

This Strategic Origination Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of   (the "Effective Date"), by and between Rare Structure LLC (the "Provider"), and   (the "Participant").

1. Purpose & Scope of Mandate

1.1 System Architecture. Provider has created, operates, and deploys proprietary data-processing architecture designed to ingest, resolve, and detect unstructured corporate inflection points, including federal contract awards and capital mobilization signals. Provider utilizes this specialized data-engineering infrastructure to identify and originate off-market commercial financing opportunities.

1.2 Strategic Origination. Participant engages Provider on a business development basis to route proprietary deal flow and commercial financing opportunities within Participant's designated underwriting verticals. This engagement is explicitly framed as an un-brokered origination channel driven by algorithmic discovery and executed with complete institutional confidentiality.

1.3 Discretion, Execution & Infrastructure Autonomy. Provider retains absolute sole discretion over the methods, data architecture, protocols, and front-facing enterprise networks utilized to execute this mandate. Provider shall bear all costs and expenses associated with the development, deployment, and maintenance of its proprietary architecture, including all computational data ingestion, high-dimensional scalar processing, vectorized entity resolution, and outbound routing infrastructure (including enterprise domains, inbox provisioning, and network warming). Participant bears no financial responsibility for Provider's operational, technical, or outreach overhead beyond the fees explicitly defined in Section 3.

1.4 Non-Brokerage & Liability Acknowledgment. The Parties acknowledge and agree that Provider acts solely as a data-routing infrastructure and lead generation service. Provider does not, and will not, advise on specific credit terms, negotiate financing structures, quote interest rates, or act as a registered commercial loan broker, broker-dealer, or financial advisor. Provider makes no representations or warranties regarding the creditworthiness, solvency, or performance of any routed entity. Participant retains sole responsibility for all underwriting, due diligence, and credit decisions.

1.5 Non-Exclusive Engagement. Participant acknowledges and agrees that this Agreement does not grant Participant exclusive rights to Provider's proprietary data pipeline, origination network, or routed deal flow. Provider retains the absolute, unrestricted right to enter into similar strategic origination agreements with other financial institutions, capital providers, and market participants, and to route any originated opportunity to any party at Provider's sole strategic discretion.

1.6 Data Accuracy & Reliance. Participant acknowledges that Provider's infrastructure synthesizes information from disparate public and third-party sources. While Provider employs best efforts to ensure signal fidelity, Provider makes no representations, warranties, or guarantees regarding the absolute accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of the routed data or catalyst events. Participant assumes sole and absolute responsibility for independently verifying all data, entity identities, and transaction parameters prior to executing any Financing Event.

2. Operational Routing Protocol

This Agreement establishes the structured routing protocol whereby Provider dedicates architectural capacity to isolate, surface, and deliver asymmetric, off-market financing opportunities to Participant.

2.1 Discretionary Discovery. Provider retains absolute engineering control over the data pipeline inputs, matching models, and threshold criteria. Routing is triggered solely when a target profile satisfies internal catalyst parameters and aligns with Participant's stated underwriting criteria.

2.2 Direct Introductions. Originated opportunities will be routed to the Participant's designated point of contact via email or other agreed-upon secure channels.

2.3 Protected Client List. Participant may, strictly concurrent with the execution of this Agreement, provide Provider with a designated "Protected Client List" consisting of Participant's existing, active borrower relationships. Provider will suppress these entities from its routing pipeline, and Participant shall owe no Success Fee for transactions executed with entities explicitly listed on the Protected Client List prior to Provider's introduction.

3. Financial Architecture & Commercial Terms

As consideration for the infrastructure deployment and strategic routing resources provided under this Agreement, Participant shall remit payments according to the following structures.

3.1 Systems Integration & Enrichment Fee. Participant shall pay a one-time, non-refundable Systems Integration Fee of  , due in full upon execution of this Agreement. Participant acknowledges this is a singular infrastructure allocation and data enrichment fee, not a recurring fee-for-service, and Provider assumes no ongoing operational obligation to execute bespoke or dedicated labor on Participant's behalf.

3.2 Marketing Referral & Success Fee. Upon the successful closing and initial funding of any credit facility, equipment loan, factoring arrangement, or other capital deployment (a "Financing Event") between Participant and an entity routed by Provider, Participant shall pay Provider a Success Fee equal to  % of the total approved facility limit or total committed capital of such Financing Event. For the avoidance of doubt, the Success Fee is calculated and owed based on the maximum approved credit limit established at closing, regardless of whether the capital is drawn down or disbursed in scheduled tranches over time. This fee is payable within   days following the execution and initial funding of the Financing Event. Once earned and remitted, the Success Fee is absolute and non-refundable. Provider assumes no liability for the subsequent performance, default, early payoff, or termination of the Financing Event, and under no circumstances shall Participant be entitled to any clawback, offset, or refund of the Success Fee.

3.3 Rolling Client Ownership. Participant explicitly agrees that Provider's Success Fee shall apply not only to the initial Financing Event, but to any and all subsequent Financing Events, renewals, line increases, or additional capital deployments executed between Participant and the Provider-introduced entity for a continuous period of twenty-four (24) months, commencing strictly from the date of Provider's initial introduction of that specific entity.

3.4 Reporting of Financing Events. Participant agrees to notify Provider in writing within five (5) business days of the execution and initial funding of any Financing Event resulting from an introduction made by Provider, detailing the total facility limit or funded amount to facilitate accurate invoicing.

3.5 Verification of Transactions. To ensure accurate accounting during the Term and the continuous twenty-four (24) month Rolling Client Ownership period defined in Section 3.3, Provider reserves the right to periodically request a status update regarding specific Provider-introduced entities. Upon reasonable written request from Provider, Participant agrees to provide, within ten (10) business days, a written certification or ledger summary detailing whether any Financing Events have been executed with the inquired entities, including the respective dates and funded amounts.

3.6 Payment Mechanics & Late Penalties. All payments owed to Provider under this Agreement shall be made in United States Dollars via wire transfer or ACH, strictly in accordance with the payment and routing instructions provided on Provider's invoice. Any amounts not paid when due shall accrue interest at a rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is less, calculated from the original due date until paid in full. Participant shall be responsible for all reasonable expenses (including attorneys' fees) incurred by Provider in collecting past due amounts.

4. Data Ingestion & Intellectual Property

4.1 Permissive Ingestion. Participant may provide Provider with underwriting matrices, target lists, or geographic preferences to calibrate Provider's active go-to-market efforts. Provider is under no contractually binding obligation to pursue or guarantee connection with any specific entity.

4.2 Intellectual Property Lock. Any lookalike models, clustering parameters, derivative enrichment data, behavioral intent signals, or analytical taxonomies generated by Provider's infrastructure remain the sole, exclusive, and unencumbered intellectual property of Provider.

5. Term & Termination

5.1 Mutual Termination. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date. Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, by providing written notice to the other Party. Participant acknowledges that the Systems Integration Fee detailed in Section 3.1 is fully earned upon execution and remains non-refundable regardless of early termination by either Party.

5.2 Survival & Tail Protection. The termination of this Agreement does not terminate Participant's financial obligations for introductions already made. Participant remains irrevocably obligated to pay Provider the Success Fees detailed in Section 3 for any and all Financing Events executed with an entity that was introduced by Provider prior to the date of termination, provided such transactions occur within the twenty-four (24) month rolling ownership period defined in Section 3.3.

6. Non-Circumvention & Confidentiality

6.1 Absolute Non-Circumvention. Participant irrevocably agrees not to circumvent, bypass, or avoid Provider, directly or indirectly, in transactions with any entity, corporate profile, or individual routed or introduced by Provider. Participant shall not execute any Financing Event with a Provider-introduced entity without remitting the Success Fees detailed in Section 3.

6.2 Confidentiality. Both parties agree to maintain the strict confidentiality of all proprietary information received from the other party. Both parties explicitly covenant that all strategic mandates, target identities, proprietary operational capabilities, underwriting criteria, and structural deal discussions remain strictly confidential and protected from public disclosure. This Section shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement.

7. Indemnification

Participant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Provider from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to Provider's outreach efforts executed specifically on behalf of Participant, provided such outreach efforts are conducted in good faith and in compliance with this Agreement. This Section shall survive termination or expiration of this Agreement.

8. Miscellaneous

8.1 Governing Law. This Agreement and all performance hereunder shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

8.2 Arbitration. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association. The arbitration shall be conducted in New York, NY, and judgment on the arbitration award may be entered in any court having jurisdiction thereof. The prevailing party in any arbitration or legal proceeding shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

8.3 Entire Agreement & Amendment. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and negotiations. It may be amended only by a written agreement signed by both parties.

8.4 Authority. Each individual signing below represents that they are duly authorized to execute this Agreement on behalf of, and to bind, the party for which they sign.
