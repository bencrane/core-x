Strategic Origination Agreement

This Strategic Origination Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into and made effective as of ____________________ (the “Effective Date”), by and between Rare Structure LLC (“Principal”), and ____________________________________________________ (d/b/a ____________________________) (“Participant”).

1. Engagement

1.1 Strategic Origination. Participant engages Principal, on a non-exclusive basis, to route proprietary introductions aligned, as assessed by Principal in its sole discretion, to Participant’s capabilities and target verticals. Principal retains absolute discretion over its methods and networks, bears its own operational costs, and owes no reporting on its internal logic. Principal acts solely as a data-routing and lead-generation service — not a broker, agency, or advisor — and makes no representation regarding any routed entity; Participant retains sole responsibility for all diligence, contracting, and commercial decisions.

1.2 Introductions. An “Introduction” is a digital facilitation connecting Participant with a representative of a third-party entity identified and selected solely by Principal’s proprietary infrastructure. Upon facilitation, Principal’s obligation for that Introduction is fully discharged; all subsequent engagement and outcomes rest solely between Participant and the counterparty.

2. Commercial Terms

2.1 Prepaid Range Allocation. Participant shall pay an upfront fee of ____________________ (the “Prepaid Fee”), due in full upon execution of this Agreement. The Prepaid Fee secures a dedicated allocation of no fewer than ________ Introductions (the “Introduction Minimum”) and up to ________ Introductions (the “Introduction Maximum”), establishing a unit price of ____________ per Introduction (the “Per-Introduction Price”, equal to the Prepaid Fee divided by the Introduction Minimum). Introductions beyond the Introduction Minimum, up to the Introduction Maximum, are included at no additional charge.

2.2 Primary Term. The window for fulfilling the allocation (the “Primary Term”) shall span ____________ days, commencing the first Monday following the later of the Effective Date and the date the Prepaid Fee irrevocably clears, before which Principal has no obligation to commence origination activity.

2.3 Ledger Settlement. If, at the expiration of the Primary Term, Principal has facilitated fewer Introductions than the Introduction Minimum, Principal shall remit to Participant a settlement equal to the shortfall below the Introduction Minimum multiplied by the Per-Introduction Price. Delivery at or above the Introduction Minimum settles the allocation in full, and no settlement is owed. Upon settlement or fulfillment, this Agreement terminates.

3. General

3.1 Each party shall keep the other’s proprietary information strictly confidential. This Agreement is governed by New York law; disputes are resolved by binding arbitration in New York, NY under AAA rules, with attorneys’ fees to the prevailing party. This is the entire agreement between the parties, amendable only in a writing signed by both. Each signatory below represents due authority to bind the party for which they sign. Participant shall indemnify Principal against claims arising from good-faith outreach executed on Participant’s behalf hereunder.
