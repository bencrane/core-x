Strategic Origination Agreement

This Strategic Origination Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into and made effective as of ____________________ (the “Effective Date”), by and between Rare Structure LLC (“Principal”), and ____________________________________________________ (d/b/a ____________________________) (“Participant”).

1. The Event

1.1 Originated Digital Event. Principal shall source the audience for, host, and deliver one live video conference of up to sixty (60) minutes (the “Event”) on Participant’s behalf, on a hosting platform provided by Principal. Principal retains sole discretion over its sourcing methods and networks, bears its own costs, and owes no reporting on its internal logic; Principal is a data-routing and event-facilitation service — not a broker, agency, or advisor — and makes no representation regarding any attendee.

1.2 Audience. Participant may submit targeting preferences, which Principal synthesizes at its sole discretion to assemble the Event audience. No personally identifying attendee information is provided before the Event.

2. Commercial Terms

2.1 Prepaid Event Fee & Attendance Range. Participant shall pay an upfront fee of ____________________ (the “Prepaid Event Fee”), due in full upon execution of this Agreement. The Prepaid Event Fee secures the Event with no fewer than ________ live attendees (the “Minimum Attendance”) and up to ________ live attendees (the “Maximum Attendance”), establishing a unit price of ____________ per attendee (the “Per-Attendee Price”, equal to the Prepaid Event Fee divided by the Minimum Attendance). Attendees beyond the Minimum Attendance, up to the Maximum Attendance, are included at no additional charge.

2.2 Scheduling. The parties shall select the Event date within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date, with the Event held no earlier than thirty (30) days after selection and no later than ninety (90) days after the Effective Date. Principal has no obligation to commence until the Prepaid Event Fee has irrevocably cleared.

2.3 Attendance Settlement. Live attendance is audited by a count taken five (5) minutes after the Event’s scheduled start. If audited attendance is below the Minimum Attendance, Principal shall refund Participant the shortfall below the Minimum Attendance multiplied by the Per-Attendee Price. Attendance at or above the Minimum Attendance settles the Event in full. Participant may request a supplemental event in lieu of the refund; granting one is at Principal’s sole discretion, and only a granted and accepted supplemental event displaces the refund.

2.4 Attendee Roster. Within ninety-six (96) hours after the Event, Principal shall deliver a roster of attendees who consented to be identified to Participant.

3. General

3.1 Each party shall keep the other’s proprietary information strictly confidential. This Agreement is governed by New York law; disputes are resolved by binding arbitration in New York, NY under AAA rules, with attorneys’ fees to the prevailing party. This is the entire agreement between the parties, amendable only in a writing signed by both. Each signatory below represents due authority to bind the party for which they sign. Participant shall indemnify Principal against claims arising from good-faith outreach executed on Participant’s behalf hereunder.
