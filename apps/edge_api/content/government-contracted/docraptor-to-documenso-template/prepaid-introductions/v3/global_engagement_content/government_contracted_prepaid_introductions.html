Strategic Origination Agreement

This Strategic Origination Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into and made effective as of ____________________ (the “Effective Date”), by and between Rare Structure LLC (“Operator”), and ______________________________ (d/b/a ____________________________) (“Principal”).

1. Core Infrastructure & Deliverables

1.1 The Routing Seat. Operator maintains and operates a proprietary, out-of-core data infrastructure and private routing network. Subject to the full execution of this Agreement and the irrevocable clearance of the Prepaid Fee, Operator grants Principal a reserved routing seat within the active network to receive a dedicated allocation of introductions.

1.2 Continuous Calibration. The reservation of this seat strictly entitles Principal to the continuous algorithmic calibration of their submitted parameters against external market signals. Principal acknowledges that Operator is actively running synthesis between Principal’s operational profile and broader market data.

2. Data Ingestion & Engine Parameters

2.1 Parameter Ingestion. Upon the clearance of the Prepaid Fee, Principal shall be granted the facility to submit their operational parameters, underwriting criteria, and market preferences (the “Capacity Profile”). Operator shall synthesize these passive inputs to continuously calibrate the routing allocation engine.

2.2 Calibration Entities. Principal’s submission may include the identities of specific market entities. Principal explicitly acknowledges and agrees that any specific entities provided are submitted strictly as illustrative data points for comparative clustering and vector alignment. Operator assumes absolutely no obligation to monitor, target, solicit, or route connections regarding any specific entity submitted within this calibration data.

2.3 Exclusion Roster & Suppression Deadline. Principal may provide Operator with an “Exclusion Roster” consisting of Principal’s existing, active accounts or relationships. To be eligible for suppression from Operator’s routing pipeline, this roster must be submitted in full no later than 3:00 PM Eastern Time on the first Sunday immediately following the Payment Date. If Principal fails to provide the Exclusion Roster prior to this strict deadline, Operator shall commence pipeline execution under the assumption of zero protected entities. Any connections facilitated to entities not explicitly listed prior to the deadline shall be fully credited against the Introduction Allocation.

3. Algorithmic Routing & Alignment

3.1 Definition of Network Counterparty. For the purposes of this Agreement, a “Network Counterparty” is defined exclusively as a third-party commercial organization, operational node, or corporate entity identified, resolved, and selected solely by Operator’s proprietary infrastructure. Principal explicitly acknowledges that Network Counterparties are sourced from Operator’s enclosed ecosystem and are not compiled, researched, or targeted at the specific direction of the Principal.

3.2 Routed Connections. A “Routed Connection” (or “Introduction”) is strictly defined as a digital facilitation connecting Principal with a representative of a Network Counterparty.

3.3 Algorithmic Alignment. Operator retains absolute engineering control over the data pipeline inputs, matching models, and threshold criteria. A connection is routed when, in Operator’s sole and strategic discretion, a Network Counterparty’s expressed or inferred operational requirements, preferences, or systemic market positioning align with the Principal’s submitted Capacity Profile.

4. Intellectual Property & Architectural Opacity

4.1 Zero Reporting Obligation. Operator shall have no obligation to disclose, explain, justify, or provide reporting on the internal algorithmic logic, scoring mechanisms, or specific data inputs utilized to determine any alignment or routing decision.

4.2 Proprietary Architecture. The underlying routing engine relies on highly opaque, out-of-core computational processing, multi-dimensional vector embeddings, and non-linear data synthesis that cannot be linearly audited or extracted. Principal acknowledges that these computational mechanics constitute strictly confidential, proprietary intellectual property intrinsic to the foundational valuation of Operator’s enterprise. Any audit, disclosure, or reverse-engineering of this architecture, whether intentional or inadvertent, would trigger severe, compounding material degradation to Operator’s market position and structural leverage.

5. Financial Architecture & Commercial Terms

5.1 Prepaid Origination Allocation. Principal shall pay a non-refundable upfront fee of ____________________ (the “Prepaid Fee”), due in full upon execution of this Agreement. The Prepaid Fee secures a dedicated allocation of ____________ Routed Connections (the “Introduction Allocation”), establishing a fixed unit cost of ____________________ per Routed Connection (the “Per-Introduction Rate”).

5.2 Primary Term & Commencement. The operational window for fulfilling the Introduction Allocation (the “Primary Term”) shall strictly span ____________ days. The “Payment Date” shall be strictly defined as the date the Prepaid Fee successfully processes and irrevocably clears into Operator’s designated account. The “Commencement Date” of the Primary Term shall default to the first Monday immediately following the later of: (a) the Effective Date, or (b) the Payment Date. Notwithstanding the foregoing default, Operator retains the unilateral and discretionary right to accelerate the Commencement Date to the first Monday following the date the payment was initiated or authorized by Principal. The interim period between the Effective Date and the Commencement Date is explicitly reserved for Operator’s proprietary data ingestion, pipeline calibration, and suppression architecture.

5.3 Term Expiration & Ledger Settlement. In the event that Operator does not facilitate the full Introduction Allocation prior to the expiration of the Primary Term, this Agreement dictates an automatic ledger settlement. Operator shall remit the prorated balance of the unfulfilled allocation, calculated strictly as the number of unfulfilled Routed Connections multiplied by the Per-Introduction Rate, back to Principal. Upon this ledger settlement, Principal’s routing seat is immediately revoked and this Agreement terminates, unless Operator explicitly elects to offer a Facility Rollover under Section 5.4.

5.4 Facility Rollover & Top-Up Privilege. The decision to extend a routing seat beyond the Primary Term remains at the sole and absolute discretion of Operator, based upon Operator’s evaluation of Principal’s operational follow-through, counterparty credibility, and network alignment. If Operator elects to offer a rollover, Principal may accept by waiving the Ledger Settlement and applying the unfulfilled balance to secure a subsequent origination term. To validate this rollover and retain the routing seat, Operator will issue a Top-Up Notice, and Principal must remit the required capital to bring the origination facility back to the baseline Prepaid Fee threshold prior to the commencement of the subsequent term.

6. Market Reality & Counterparty Friction

6.1 Market Volatility & Autonomy. Principal explicitly acknowledges that Operator’s infrastructure functions within a dynamic, highly unpredictable market environment. Operator makes no representations or warranties regarding macroeconomic conditions, transaction volume, or the velocity of market signals.

6.2 Counterparty Friction. Operator strictly facilitates digital introductions; Operator exercises no control over the autonomy, responsiveness, or ultimate intent of any Network Counterparty. Should algorithmic alignment occur, but the Network Counterparty declines engagement, ceases communication, or refuses facilitation, such events constitute standard market friction. Operator is completely absolved of any liability or obligation regarding the ultimate responsiveness, communication, or conversion of any Routed Connection.

7. Miscellaneous & Protections

7.1 Confidentiality. Both parties agree to maintain the strict confidentiality of all proprietary information received from the other party. Both parties explicitly covenant that all strategic initiatives, network identities, proprietary operational capabilities, and structural discussions remain strictly confidential and protected from public disclosure.

7.2 Governing Law & Arbitration. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York. Any disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association in New York, NY.
