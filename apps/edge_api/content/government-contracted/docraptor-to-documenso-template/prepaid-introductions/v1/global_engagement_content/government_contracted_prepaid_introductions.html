RARE STRUCTURE

Strategic Origination Agreement

This Strategic Origination Agreement (the “Agreement”), effective as of ____________ (the “Effective Date”), is entered into by Rare Structure LLC (the “Provider”) and the participating entity, ______________________________ d/b/a ____________________________, hereby defined as the “Participant”.

1. Purpose & Scope

1.1 System Architecture. Provider has created, operates, and deploys proprietary data-processing architecture designed to ingest, resolve, and detect unstructured corporate inflection points, including federal contract awards and prime contractor ecosystem signals. Provider utilizes this specialized data-engineering infrastructure to identify and originate off-market commercial partnership and subcontracting opportunities.

1.2 Strategic Origination. Participant engages Provider on a business development basis to route proprietary introductions aligned, as assessed by Provider, to the Participant’s capabilities, operational parameters, and target verticals. This engagement is explicitly framed as an un-brokered origination channel driven by algorithmic discovery configured, applied, and acted upon at Provider’s sole and strategic discretion.

1.3 Discretion, Execution & Infrastructure Autonomy. Provider retains absolute sole discretion over the methods, data architecture, protocols, and front-facing enterprise networks utilized to execute this Agreement. Provider shall bear all costs and expenses associated with the development, deployment, and maintenance of its proprietary architecture, including all computational data ingestion, high-dimensional scalar processing, vectorized entity resolution, and outbound routing infrastructure. Participant bears no financial responsibility for Provider’s operational, technical, or outreach overhead beyond the fees explicitly defined in Section 3.

1.4 Non-Brokerage & Liability Acknowledgment. The Parties acknowledge and agree that Provider acts solely as a data-routing infrastructure and lead generation service. Provider does not, and will not, advise on specific contract terms, negotiate teaming agreements, or act as a registered employment agency, staffing broker, or legal advisor. Provider makes no representations or warranties regarding the viability, solvency, or intent of any routed entity. Participant retains sole responsibility for all due diligence, contracting, and operational decisions.

1.5 Non-Exclusive Engagement. Participant acknowledges and agrees that this Agreement does not grant Participant exclusive rights to Provider’s proprietary data pipeline, origination network, or routed deal flow. Provider retains the absolute, unrestricted right to enter into similar strategic origination agreements with other market participants, including competing firms, and to route any originated opportunity to any party at Provider’s sole strategic discretion.

1.6 Data Accuracy & Reliance. Participant acknowledges that Provider’s infrastructure synthesizes information from disparate public and third-party sources. While Provider employs best efforts to ensure signal fidelity, Provider makes no representations, warranties, or guarantees regarding the absolute accuracy, completeness, or timeliness of the routed data.

2. Operational Routing Protocol

2.1 Discretionary Discovery. Provider retains absolute engineering control over the data pipeline inputs, matching models, and threshold criteria. Routing is triggered when, in Provider’s sole and strategic discretion, a target profile satisfies internal catalyst parameters and reflects a workable fit. Such algorithmic and model-driven discovery is a tool Provider applies in its sole discretion; it does not bind, predetermine, or guarantee any routing decision.

2.2 Direct Introductions. Originated opportunities will be routed to the Participant’s designated point of contact. For the purposes of this Agreement, an “Introduction” is strictly defined as an outbound email facilitation connecting Participant with a representative of a target entity.

2.3 Protected Client List. Participant may, strictly concurrent with the execution of this Agreement, provide Provider with a designated “Protected Client List” consisting of Participant’s existing, active relationships. Provider will suppress these entities from its routing pipeline, and Participant shall not be charged an Introduction allocation for connections made with entities explicitly listed on the Protected Client List prior to Provider’s initial outreach.

3. Financial Architecture & Commercial Terms

3.1 Prepaid Origination Allocation. Participant shall pay a non-refundable upfront fee of {{ amount }}, due in full upon execution of this Agreement. This payment secures a dedicated allocation of {{ introductions }} Introductions, establishing a fixed unit cost of {{ price_per_introduction }} per Introduction.

3.2 Primary Fulfillment Term. Provider shall have a primary operational window of {{ term_days }} days from the Effective Date (the “Primary Term”) to execute and deliver the {{ introductions }} Introductions.

3.3 Condition Precedent to Commencement. Provider shall have no obligation to commence origination activity under this Agreement until the payment set forth in Section 3.1 has been received and irrevocably cleared in full.

3.4 Term Expiration & Resolution. In the event that Provider does not facilitate the full allocation of {{ introductions }} Introductions prior to the expiration of the Primary Term, Participant shall be immediately presented with a binary resolution choice for the unfulfilled balance:

Option A (Prorated Refund): Participant may elect to receive a cash refund for the unfulfilled Introductions, calculated precisely as the number of unfulfilled Introductions multiplied by {{ price_per_introduction }}.

Option B (Credit Rollover): Participant may elect to convert the unfulfilled balance into a retained allocation credit, locking in the {{ price_per_introduction }} rate for future Introductions.

3.5 Rollover Mechanics & Passive Allocation. If Participant elects Option B (Credit Rollover) under Section 3.4, the relationship formally transitions into the “Rollover Period.” During the Rollover Period, Participant retains a right to receive their remaining allocation of Introductions. However, Participant explicitly acknowledges and agrees that during the Rollover Period, Provider is under zero obligation to conduct dedicated outbound campaigns, maintain active sourcing operations on Participant’s behalf, or fulfill the remaining credits within any specific timeframe. Provider’s obligation converts entirely to a passive allocation mandate, wherein remaining credits will be fulfilled strictly at Provider’s sole discretion as organic, relevant opportunities arise through Provider’s general market operations.

4. Data Ingestion & Intellectual Property

4.1 Permissive Ingestion. Participant may provide Provider with targeting matrices or operational preferences to calibrate Provider’s active go-to-market efforts.

4.2 Intellectual Property Lock. Any lookalike models, clustering parameters, derivative enrichment data, behavioral intent signals, or analytical taxonomies generated by Provider’s infrastructure remain the sole, exclusive, and unencumbered intellectual property of Provider.

5. Confidentiality

5.1 Confidentiality. Both parties agree to maintain the strict confidentiality of all proprietary information received from the other party. Both parties explicitly covenant that all strategic initiatives, target identities, proprietary operational capabilities, and structural discussions remain strictly confidential and protected from public disclosure.

6. Indemnification

Participant agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Provider from and against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys’ fees) arising out of or related to Provider’s outreach efforts executed specifically on behalf of Participant, provided such outreach efforts are conducted in good faith and in compliance with this Agreement.

7. Miscellaneous

7.1 Governing Law. This Agreement and all performance hereunder shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of New York, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

7.2 Arbitration. Any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration in accordance with the rules of the American Arbitration Association. The arbitration shall be conducted in New York, NY, and judgment on the arbitration award may be entered in any court having jurisdiction thereof. The prevailing party in any arbitration or legal proceeding shall be entitled to recover its reasonable attorneys’ fees and costs.

7.3 Entire Agreement & Amendment. This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, understandings, and negotiations. It may be amended only by a written agreement signed by both parties.
