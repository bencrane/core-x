# Strategic Origination Agreement

This Strategic Origination Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of {{effective_date}} (the "Effective Date"), by and between Rare Structure LLC, doing business as Active Operators (the "Provider"), and {{participant_name}} (the "Participant").

## 1. Purpose & Scope of Mandate

**1.1 System Architecture.** Provider has created, operates, and deploys proprietary data-processing architecture designed to ingest, resolve, and detect unstructured corporate inflection points. Provider utilizes this specialized data-engineering infrastructure and sourcing engine to identify and originate off-market commercial deal flow.

**1.2 Strategic Origination.** Participant engages Provider on a business development basis to route proprietary commercial and service contract opportunities within Participant's designated service verticals. This engagement is explicitly framed as an un-brokered origination channel driven by algorithmic discovery and executed with complete institutional confidentiality.

**1.3 Discretion & Execution.** Provider retains absolute sole discretion over the methods, data architecture, protocols, and front-facing enterprise networks, brands, or affiliate channels utilized to execute this mandate.

**1.4 Non-Brokerage Acknowledgment.** The Parties acknowledge and agree that Provider acts solely as a finder and data-routing infrastructure. Provider does not, and will not, advise on specific deal terms, negotiate transaction structures, quote explicit financing offers, or act as a registered broker-dealer or licensed broker in any capacity.

## 2. Operational Routing Protocol

This Agreement establishes the structured routing protocol whereby Provider dedicates architectural capacity to isolate, surface, and deliver asymmetric, off-market business development opportunities to Participant, consistent with Participant's capabilities.

**2.1 Discretionary Discovery.** Provider retains absolute engineering control over the data pipeline inputs, matching models, and threshold criteria. Routing is triggered solely when a target profile satisfies internal catalyst parameters.

**2.2 Direct Introductions.** Pending the expressed opt-in by target profiles, originated opportunities will be routed to the Participant's designated point of contact via email or other agreed-upon channels.

**2.3 Zero-Spec Exclusivity.** Participant acknowledges that while specific transaction parameters will be informed by Participant's provided preferences and criteria, Provider will ultimately make routing decisions according to its internal and proprietary protocol, which will remain private and undisclosed.

## 3. Financial Architecture & Commercial Terms

As consideration for the infrastructure deployment and strategic routing resources provided under this Agreement, Participant shall remit payments according to the following structures. The economic parameters governing this routing infrastructure are structured to preserve transaction status and align long-term platform performance.

**3.1 Engagement Fee.** Participant shall pay a non-refundable Engagement Fee of {{term_fee}} for the Initial Term, due in full upon execution of this Agreement.

**3.2 Nature of Fee.** This fee is fully earned by Provider upon receipt and is non-refundable. It represents a dedicated sourcing capacity allocation within Provider's broader network architecture. This capital underwrites continuous data-engineering overhead, infrastructure costs, signal detection, and strategic calibration. Participant acknowledges that Provider executes market outreach efforts via its own independently operated brands at its sole cost, risk, and discretion. This fee is strictly to maintain capacity allocation and is entirely independent of specific outreach volumes or transaction execution.

**3.3 Success Fee.** Upon the execution of any Deal between Participant and a target profile routed by Provider, Participant shall pay Provider a Success Fee equal to the greater of (a) {{flat_deal_fee_amount}} or (b) {{percentage_deal_fee}}% of the Total Contract Value of such Deal, payable within {{payment_window_days}} days following execution of such Deal.

**4.2 Intellectual Property Lock.** Any lookalike models, clustering parameters, derivative enrichment data, behavioral intent signals, or analytical taxonomies generated by Provider's infrastructure—regardless of whether they utilize, reference, or ingest Participant-provided data—remain the sole, exclusive, and unencumbered intellectual property of Provider.
